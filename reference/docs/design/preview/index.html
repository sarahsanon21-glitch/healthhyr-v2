A little clarity goes a long way.

Do I need a subscription to post a job?

No. The pay-per-post option lets you publish a job for $2.50 without choosing a monthly subscription.

How much is the monthly subscription?

The monthly subscription is shown at $39 per month. Included posting allowances and plan benefits will be confirmed before checkout is connected.

Who is HealthHyr for?

HealthHyr connects healthcare job seekers, including caregivers, PCAs, HHAs, CNAs, and nurses, with employers such as home care agencies, hospitals, clinics, and nursing homes.

Can I apply to the jobs in this preview?

These listings demonstrate the new design. Applications, employer accounts, and payments will need to be connected to the HealthHyr platform.
